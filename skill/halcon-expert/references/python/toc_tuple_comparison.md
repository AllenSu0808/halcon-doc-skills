## Comparison

#### List of Operators

- [`tuple_equal`](tuple_equal.md) — Test whether two tuples are equal.

- [`tuple_equal_elem`](tuple_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether two tuples are elementwise equal.

- [`tuple_greater`](tuple_greater.md) — Test whether a tuple is greater than another tuple.

- [`tuple_greater_elem`](tuple_greater_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise greater than another tuple.

- [`tuple_greater_equal`](tuple_greater_equal.md) — Test whether a tuple is greater or equal to another tuple.

- [`tuple_greater_equal_elem`](tuple_greater_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise greater or equal to another tuple.

- [`tuple_less`](tuple_less.md) — Test whether a tuple is less than another tuple.

- [`tuple_less_elem`](tuple_less_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise less than another tuple.

- [`tuple_less_equal`](tuple_less_equal.md) — Test whether a tuple is less or equal to another tuple.

- [`tuple_less_equal_elem`](tuple_less_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise less or equal to another tuple.

- [`tuple_not_equal`](tuple_not_equal.md) — Test whether two tuples are not equal.

- [`tuple_not_equal_elem`](tuple_not_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether two tuples are elementwise not equal.
