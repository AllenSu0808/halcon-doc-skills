## Type Conversion

#### List of Operators

- [`complex_to_real`](complex_to_real.md) — Convert a complex image into two real images.

- [`convert_image_type`](convert_image_type.md) — Convert the type of an image.

- [`real_to_complex`](real_to_complex.md) — Convert two real images into a complex image.

- [`real_to_vector_field`](real_to_vector_field.md) — Convert two real-valued images into a vector field image.

- [`vector_field_to_real`](vector_field_to_real.md) — Convert a vector field image into two real-valued images.
