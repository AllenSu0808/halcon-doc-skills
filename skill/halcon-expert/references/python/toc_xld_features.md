## Features

#### List of Operators

- [`area_center_points_xld`](area_center_points_xld.md) — Area and center of gravity (centroid) of contours and polygons
  treated as point clouds.

- [`area_center_xld`](area_center_xld.md) — Area and center of gravity (centroid) of contours and polygons.

- [`circularity_xld`](circularity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the circularity (similarity to a circle) of contours or
  polygons.

- [`compactness_xld`](compactness_xld.md) — Shape factor for the compactness of contours or polygons.

- [`contour_point_num_xld`](contour_point_num_xld.md) — Return the number of points in an XLD contour.

- [`convexity_xld`](convexity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the convexity of contours or polygons.

- [`diameter_xld`](diameter_xld.md) — Maximum distance between two contour or polygon points.

- [`dist_ellipse_contour_points_xld`](dist_ellipse_contour_points_xld.md) — Compute the distances of all contour points to an ellipse.

- [`dist_ellipse_contour_xld`](dist_ellipse_contour_xld.md) — Compute the distance of contours to an ellipse.

- [`dist_rectangle2_contour_points_xld`](dist_rectangle2_contour_points_xld.md) — Compute the distances of all contour points to a rectangle.

- [`eccentricity_points_xld`](eccentricity_points_xld.md) — Anisometry of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`eccentricity_xld`](eccentricity_xld.md) — Shape features derived from the ellipse parameters of contours or polygons.

- [`elliptic_axis_points_xld`](elliptic_axis_points_xld.md) — Parameters of the equivalent ellipse of contours or polygons treated as
  point clouds.

- [`elliptic_axis_xld`](elliptic_axis_xld.md) — Parameters of the equivalent ellipse of contours or polygons.

- [`fit_circle_contour_xld`](fit_circle_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by circles.

- [`fit_ellipse_contour_xld`](fit_ellipse_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by ellipses or elliptic arcs.

- [`fit_line_contour_xld`](fit_line_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by line segments.

- [`fit_rectangle2_contour_xld`](fit_rectangle2_contour_xld.md) — Fit rectangles to XLD contours.

- [`get_contour_angle_xld`](get_contour_angle_xld.md) — Calculate the direction of an XLD contour for each contour point.

- [`get_contour_attrib_xld`](get_contour_attrib_xld.md) — Return point attribute values of an XLD contour.

- [`get_contour_global_attrib_xld`](get_contour_global_attrib_xld.md) — Return global attributes values of an XLD contour.

- [`get_regress_params_xld`](get_regress_params_xld.md) — Return XLD contour parameters.

- [`height_width_ratio_xld`](height_width_ratio_xld.md) — Compute the width, height, and aspect ratio of the enclosing
  rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes of contours or polygons.

- [`info_parallels_xld`](info_parallels_xld.md) — Return information about the gray values of the area enclosed by XLD
  parallels.

- [`length_xld`](length_xld.md) — Length of contours or polygons.

- [`local_max_contours_xld`](local_max_contours_xld.md) — Select XLD contours with a local maximum of gray values.

- [`max_parallels_xld`](max_parallels_xld.md) — Join modified XLD parallels lying on the same polygon.

- [`moments_any_points_xld`](moments_any_points_xld.md) — Arbitrary geometric moments of contours or polygons treated as point
  clouds.

- [`moments_any_xld`](moments_any_xld.md) — Arbitrary geometric moments of contours or polygons.

- [`moments_points_xld`](moments_points_xld.md) — Geometric moments M20, M02, and M11
  of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`moments_xld`](moments_xld.md) — Geometric moments M20, M02, and
  M11 of contours or polygons.

- [`orientation_points_xld`](orientation_points_xld.md) — Calculate the orientation of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`orientation_xld`](orientation_xld.md) — Calculate the orientation of contours or polygons.

- [`query_contour_attribs_xld`](query_contour_attribs_xld.md) — Return the names of the defined attributes of an XLD contour.

- [`query_contour_global_attribs_xld`](query_contour_global_attribs_xld.md) — Return the names of the defined global attributes of an XLD contour.

- [`rectangularity_xld`](rectangularity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the rectangularity of contours or polygons.

- [`select_contours_xld`](select_contours_xld.md) — Select XLD contours according to several features.

- [`select_shape_xld`](select_shape_xld.md) — Select contours or polygons using shape features.

- [`select_xld_point`](select_xld_point.md) — Choose all contours or polygons containing a given point.

- [`smallest_circle_xld`](smallest_circle_xld.md) — Smallest enclosing circle of contours or polygons.

- [`smallest_rectangle1_xld`](smallest_rectangle1_xld.md) — Enclosing rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes of contours or polygons.

- [`smallest_rectangle2_xld`](smallest_rectangle2_xld.md) — Smallest enclosing rectangle with arbitrary orientation of contours or
  polygons.

- [`test_closed_xld`](test_closed_xld.md) — Test whether contours or polygons are closed.

- [`test_self_intersection_xld`](test_self_intersection_xld.md) — Test XLD contours or polygons for self intersection.

- [`test_xld_point`](test_xld_point.md) — Test whether one or more contours or polygons enclose the given point(s).
