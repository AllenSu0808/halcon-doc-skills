## Arithmetic

#### List of Operators

- [`tuple_abs`](tuple_abs.md) — Compute the absolute value of a tuple.

- [`tuple_acos`](tuple_acos.md) — Compute the arccosine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_acosh`](tuple_acosh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic cosine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_add`](tuple_add.md) — Add two tuples.

- [`tuple_asin`](tuple_asin.md) — Compute the arcsine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_asinh`](tuple_asinh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic sine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_atan`](tuple_atan.md) — Compute the arctangent of a tuple.

- [`tuple_atan2`](tuple_atan2.md) — Compute the arctangent of a tuple for all four quadrants.

- [`tuple_atanh`](tuple_atanh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic tangent of a tuple.

- [`tuple_cbrt`](tuple_cbrt.md) — Compute the cube root of a tuple.

- [`tuple_ceil`](tuple_ceil.md) — Compute the ceiling function of a tuple.

- [`tuple_cos`](tuple_cos.md) — Compute the cosine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_cosh`](tuple_cosh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic cosine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_cumul`](tuple_cumul.md) — Calculate the cumulative sums of a tuple.

- [`tuple_deg`](tuple_deg.md) — Convert a tuple from radians to degrees.

- [`tuple_div`](tuple_div.md) — Divide two tuples.

- [`tuple_erf`](tuple_erf.md) — Compute the error function of a tuple.

- [`tuple_erfc`](tuple_erfc.md) — Compute the complementary error function of a tuple.

- [`tuple_exp`](tuple_exp.md) — Compute the exponential of a tuple.

- [`tuple_exp10`](tuple_exp10.md) — Compute the base 10 exponential of a tuple.

- [`tuple_exp2`](tuple_exp2.md) — Compute the base 2 exponential of a tuple.

- [`tuple_fabs`](tuple_fabs.md) — Compute the absolute value of a tuple (as floating point numbers).

- [`tuple_floor`](tuple_floor.md) — Compute the floor function of a tuple.

- [`tuple_fmod`](tuple_fmod.md) — Calculate the remainder of the floating point division of two tuples.

- [`tuple_hypot`](tuple_hypot.md) — Calculate the hypotenuse of two tuples.

- [`tuple_ldexp`](tuple_ldexp.md) — Calculate the ldexp function of two tuples.

- [`tuple_lgamma`](tuple_lgamma.md) — Compute the logarithm of the absolute value of the gamma function of
  a tuple.

- [`tuple_log`](tuple_log.md) — Compute the natural logarithm of a tuple.

- [`tuple_log10`](tuple_log10.md) — Compute the base 10 logarithm of a tuple.

- [`tuple_log2`](tuple_log2.md) — Compute the base 2 logarithm of a tuple.

- [`tuple_max2`](tuple_max2.md) — Calculate the elementwise maximum of two tuples.

- [`tuple_min2`](tuple_min2.md) — Calculate the elementwise minimum of two tuples.

- [`tuple_mod`](tuple_mod.md) — Calculate the remainder of the integer division of two tuples.

- [`tuple_mult`](tuple_mult.md) — Multiply two tuples.

- [`tuple_neg`](tuple_neg.md) — Negate a tuple.

- [`tuple_pow`](tuple_pow.md) — Calculate the power function of two tuples.

- [`tuple_rad`](tuple_rad.md) — Convert a tuple from degrees to radians.

- [`tuple_sgn`](tuple_sgn.md) — Calculate the sign of a tuple.

- [`tuple_sin`](tuple_sin.md) — Compute the sine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_sinh`](tuple_sinh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic sine of a tuple.

- [`tuple_sqrt`](tuple_sqrt.md) — Compute the square root of a tuple.

- [`tuple_sub`](tuple_sub.md) — Subtract two tuples.

- [`tuple_tan`](tuple_tan.md) — Compute the tangent of a tuple.

- [`tuple_tanh`](tuple_tanh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic tangent of a tuple.

- [`tuple_tgamma`](tuple_tgamma.md) — Compute the gamma function of a tuple.
