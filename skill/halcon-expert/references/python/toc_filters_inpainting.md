## Inpainting

#### List of Operators

- [`harmonic_interpolation`](harmonic_interpolation.md) — Perform a harmonic interpolation on an image region.

- [`inpainting_aniso`](inpainting_aniso.md) — Perform an inpainting by anisotropic diffusion.

- [`inpainting_ced`](inpainting_ced.md) — Perform an inpainting by coherence enhancing diffusion.

- [`inpainting_ct`](inpainting_ct.md) — Perform an inpainting by coherence transport.

- [`inpainting_mcf`](inpainting_mcf.md) — Perform an inpainting by smoothing of level lines.

- [`inpainting_texture`](inpainting_texture.md) — Perform an inpainting by texture propagation.
