## Conversion

#### List of Operators

- [`HandleToInteger`](handle_to_integer.md) — Convert a handle into an integer.

- [`IntegerToHandle`](integer_to_handle.md) — Convert an integer into a handle.

- [`TupleChr`](tuple_chr.md) — Convert a tuple of integer numbers into strings.

- [`TupleChrt`](tuple_chrt.md) — Convert a tuple of integer numbers into strings.

- [`TupleInt`](tuple_int.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`TupleNumber`](tuple_number.md) — Convert a tuple (of strings) into a tuple of numbers.

- [`TupleOrd`](tuple_ord.md) — Convert a tuple of strings of length 1 into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`TupleOrds`](tuple_ords.md) — Convert a tuple of strings into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`TupleReal`](tuple_real.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of floating point numbers.

- [`TupleRound`](tuple_round.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`TupleString`](tuple_string.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of strings.
