## Arithmetic

#### List of Operators

- [`TupleAbs`](tuple_abs.md) — Compute the absolute value of a tuple.

- [`TupleAcos`](tuple_acos.md) — Compute the arccosine of a tuple.

- [`TupleAcosh`](tuple_acosh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic cosine of a tuple.

- [`TupleAdd`](tuple_add.md) — Add two tuples.

- [`TupleAsin`](tuple_asin.md) — Compute the arcsine of a tuple.

- [`TupleAsinh`](tuple_asinh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic sine of a tuple.

- [`TupleAtan`](tuple_atan.md) — Compute the arctangent of a tuple.

- [`TupleAtan2`](tuple_atan2.md) — Compute the arctangent of a tuple for all four quadrants.

- [`TupleAtanh`](tuple_atanh.md) — Compute the inverse hyperbolic tangent of a tuple.

- [`TupleCbrt`](tuple_cbrt.md) — Compute the cube root of a tuple.

- [`TupleCeil`](tuple_ceil.md) — Compute the ceiling function of a tuple.

- [`TupleCos`](tuple_cos.md) — Compute the cosine of a tuple.

- [`TupleCosh`](tuple_cosh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic cosine of a tuple.

- [`TupleCumul`](tuple_cumul.md) — Calculate the cumulative sums of a tuple.

- [`TupleDeg`](tuple_deg.md) — Convert a tuple from radians to degrees.

- [`TupleDiv`](tuple_div.md) — Divide two tuples.

- [`TupleErf`](tuple_erf.md) — Compute the error function of a tuple.

- [`TupleErfc`](tuple_erfc.md) — Compute the complementary error function of a tuple.

- [`TupleExp10`](tuple_exp10.md) — Compute the base 10 exponential of a tuple.

- [`TupleExp2`](tuple_exp2.md) — Compute the base 2 exponential of a tuple.

- [`TupleFloor`](tuple_floor.md) — Compute the floor function of a tuple.

- [`TupleFmod`](tuple_fmod.md) — Calculate the remainder of the floating point division of two tuples.

- [`TupleHypot`](tuple_hypot.md) — Calculate the hypotenuse of two tuples.

- [`TupleLdexp`](tuple_ldexp.md) — Calculate the ldexp function of two tuples.

- [`TupleLgamma`](tuple_lgamma.md) — Compute the logarithm of the absolute value of the gamma function of
  a tuple.

- [`TupleLog`](tuple_log.md) — Compute the natural logarithm of a tuple.

- [`TupleLog10`](tuple_log10.md) — Compute the base 10 logarithm of a tuple.

- [`TupleLog2`](tuple_log2.md) — Compute the base 2 logarithm of a tuple.

- [`TupleMax2`](tuple_max2.md) — Calculate the elementwise maximum of two tuples.

- [`TupleMin2`](tuple_min2.md) — Calculate the elementwise minimum of two tuples.

- [`TupleMod`](tuple_mod.md) — Calculate the remainder of the integer division of two tuples.

- [`TupleMult`](tuple_mult.md) — Multiply two tuples.

- [`TupleNeg`](tuple_neg.md) — Negate a tuple.

- [`TuplePow`](tuple_pow.md) — Calculate the power function of two tuples.

- [`TupleRad`](tuple_rad.md) — Convert a tuple from degrees to radians.

- [`TupleSgn`](tuple_sgn.md) — Calculate the sign of a tuple.

- [`TupleSin`](tuple_sin.md) — Compute the sine of a tuple.

- [`TupleSinh`](tuple_sinh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic sine of a tuple.

- [`TupleSqrt`](tuple_sqrt.md) — Compute the square root of a tuple.

- [`TupleSub`](tuple_sub.md) — Subtract two tuples.

- [`TupleTan`](tuple_tan.md) — Compute the tangent of a tuple.

- [`TupleTanh`](tuple_tanh.md) — Compute the hyperbolic tangent of a tuple.

- [`TupleTgamma`](tuple_tgamma.md) — Compute the gamma function of a tuple.
