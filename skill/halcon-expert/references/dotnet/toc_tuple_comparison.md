## Comparison

#### List of Operators

- [`TupleEqual`](tuple_equal.md) — Test whether two tuples are equal.

- [`TupleEqualElem`](tuple_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether two tuples are elementwise equal.

- [`TupleGreater`](tuple_greater.md) — Test whether a tuple is greater than another tuple.

- [`TupleGreaterElem`](tuple_greater_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise greater than another tuple.

- [`TupleGreaterEqual`](tuple_greater_equal.md) — Test whether a tuple is greater or equal to another tuple.

- [`TupleGreaterEqualElem`](tuple_greater_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise greater or equal to another tuple.

- [`TupleLess`](tuple_less.md) — Test whether a tuple is less than another tuple.

- [`TupleLessElem`](tuple_less_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise less than another tuple.

- [`TupleLessEqual`](tuple_less_equal.md) — Test whether a tuple is less or equal to another tuple.

- [`TupleLessEqualElem`](tuple_less_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether a tuple is elementwise less or equal to another tuple.

- [`TupleNotEqual`](tuple_not_equal.md) — Test whether two tuples are not equal.

- [`TupleNotEqualElem`](tuple_not_equal_elem.md) — Test, whether two tuples are elementwise not equal.
