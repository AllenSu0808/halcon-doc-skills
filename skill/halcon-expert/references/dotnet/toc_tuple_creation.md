## Creation

#### List of Operators

- [`ClearHandle`](clear_handle.md) — Clear the content of a handle.

- [`TupleConcat`](tuple_concat.md) — Concatenate two tuples to a new one.

- [`TupleConstant`](tuple_constant.md) — Generate a tuple with the value of a HDevelop language constant.

- [`TupleGenConst`](tuple_gen_const.md) — Generate a tuple of a specific length and initialize its elements.

- [`TupleGenSequence`](tuple_gen_sequence.md) — Generate a tuple with a sequence of equidistant values.

- [`TupleRand`](tuple_rand.md) — Return a tuple of random numbers between 0 and 1.

- [`TupleRepeat`](tuple_repeat.md) — Repeat a tuple.

- [`TupleRepeatElem`](tuple_repeat_elem.md) — Repeat the elements of a tuple.
