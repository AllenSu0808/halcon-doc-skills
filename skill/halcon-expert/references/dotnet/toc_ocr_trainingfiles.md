## Training Files

#### List of Operators

- [`AppendOcrTrainf`](append_ocr_trainf.md) — Add characters to a training file.

- [`ConcatOcrTrainf`](concat_ocr_trainf.md) — Concat training files.

- [`ProtectOcrTrainf`](protect_ocr_trainf.md) — Protection of training data.

- [`ReadOcrTrainf`](read_ocr_trainf.md) — Read training characters from files and convert to images.

- [`ReadOcrTrainfNames`](read_ocr_trainf_names.md) — Query which characters are stored in a training file.

- [`ReadOcrTrainfNamesProtected`](read_ocr_trainf_names_protected.md) — Query which characters are stored in a (protected) training file.

- [`ReadOcrTrainfSelect`](read_ocr_trainf_select.md) — Read training specific characters from files and convert to images.

- [`WriteOcrTrainf`](write_ocr_trainf.md) — Storing of training characters into a file.

- [`WriteOcrTrainfImage`](write_ocr_trainf_image.md) — Write characters into a training file.
