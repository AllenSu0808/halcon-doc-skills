## Serial

#### List of Operators

- [`ClearSerial`](clear_serial.md) — Clear the buffer of a serial connection.

- [`CloseSerial`](close_serial.md) — Close a serial device.

- [`GetSerialParam`](get_serial_param.md) — Get the parameters of a serial device.

- [`OpenSerial`](open_serial.md) — Open a serial device.

- [`ReadSerial`](read_serial.md) — Read from a serial device.

- [`SetSerialParam`](set_serial_param.md) — Set the parameters of a serial device.

- [`WriteSerial`](write_serial.md) — Write to a serial connection.
