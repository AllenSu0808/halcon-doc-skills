## Bit Operations

#### List of Operators

- [`TupleBand`](tuple_band.md) — Compute the bitwise and of two tuples.

- [`TupleBnot`](tuple_bnot.md) — Compute the bitwise not of a tuple.

- [`TupleBor`](tuple_bor.md) — Compute the bitwise or of two tuples.

- [`TupleBxor`](tuple_bxor.md) — Compute the bitwise exclusive or of two tuples.

- [`TupleLsh`](tuple_lsh.md) — Shift a tuple bitwise to the left.

- [`TupleRsh`](tuple_rsh.md) — Shift a tuple bitwise to the right.
