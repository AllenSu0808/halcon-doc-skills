## Camera Parameters

- [`CamMatToCamPar`](cam_mat_to_cam_par.md) — Compute the internal camera parameters from a camera matrix.

- [`ReadCamPar`](read_cam_par.md) — Read internal camera parameters from a file.

- [`SerializeCamPar`](serialize_cam_par.md) — Serialize the internal camera parameters.

- [`WriteCamPar`](write_cam_par.md) — Write internal camera parameters into a file.
