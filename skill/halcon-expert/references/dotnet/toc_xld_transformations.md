## Transformations

#### List of Operators

- [`AddNoiseWhiteContourXld`](add_noise_white_contour_xld.md) — Add noise to XLD contours.

- [`ClipContoursXld`](clip_contours_xld.md) — Clip an XLD contour.

- [`ClipEndPointsContoursXld`](clip_end_points_contours_xld.md) — Clip the end points of an XLD contour.

- [`CloseContoursXld`](close_contours_xld.md) — Close an XLD contour.

- [`CombineRoadsXld`](combine_roads_xld.md) — Combine road hypotheses from two resolution levels.

- [`CropContoursXld`](crop_contours_xld.md) — Crop an XLD contour.

- [`MergeContLineScanXld`](merge_cont_line_scan_xld.md) — Merge XLD contours from successive line scan images.

- [`RegressContoursXld`](regress_contours_xld.md) — Calculate the parameters of a regression line to an XLD contour.

- [`SegmentContourAttribXld`](segment_contour_attrib_xld.md) — Segment XLD contour parts whose local attributes fulfill given
  conditions.

- [`SegmentContoursXld`](segment_contours_xld.md) — Segment XLD contours into line segments and circular or elliptic arcs.

- [`ShapeTransXld`](shape_trans_xld.md) — Transform the shape of contours or polygons.

- [`SmoothContoursXld`](smooth_contours_xld.md) — Smooth an XLD contour.

- [`SortContoursXld`](sort_contours_xld.md) — Sort contours with respect to their relative position.

- [`SplitContoursXld`](split_contours_xld.md) — Split XLD contours at dominant points.

- [`UnionAdjacentContoursXld`](union_adjacent_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of contours whose end points are close together.

- [`UnionCocircularContoursXld`](union_cocircular_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of contours that belong to the same circle.

- [`UnionCollinearContoursExtXld`](union_collinear_contours_ext_xld.md) — Compute the union of collinear contours
  (operator with extended functionality).

- [`UnionCollinearContoursXld`](union_collinear_contours_xld.md) — Unite approximately collinear contours.

- [`UnionCotangentialContoursXld`](union_cotangential_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of cotangential contours.

- [`UnionStraightContoursXld`](union_straight_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of neighboring straight contours that have a similar
  direction.
