## Bit

#### List of Operators

- [`BitAnd`](bit_and.md) — Bit-by-bit AND of all pixels of the input images.

- [`BitLshift`](bit_lshift.md) — Left shift of all pixels of the image.

- [`BitMask`](bit_mask.md) — Logical “AND” of each pixel using a bit mask.

- [`BitNot`](bit_not.md) — Complement all bits of the pixels.

- [`BitOr`](bit_or.md) — Bit-by-bit OR of all pixels of the input images.

- [`BitRshift`](bit_rshift.md) — Right shift of all pixels of the image.

- [`BitSlice`](bit_slice.md) — Extract a bit from the pixels.

- [`BitXor`](bit_xor.md) — Bit-by-bit XOR of all pixels of the input images.
