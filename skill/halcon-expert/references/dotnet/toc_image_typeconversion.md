## Type Conversion

#### List of Operators

- [`ComplexToReal`](complex_to_real.md) — Convert a complex image into two real images.

- [`ConvertImageType`](convert_image_type.md) — Convert the type of an image.

- [`RealToComplex`](real_to_complex.md) — Convert two real images into a complex image.

- [`RealToVectorField`](real_to_vector_field.md) — Convert two real-valued images into a vector field image.

- [`VectorFieldToReal`](vector_field_to_real.md) — Convert a vector field image into two real-valued images.
