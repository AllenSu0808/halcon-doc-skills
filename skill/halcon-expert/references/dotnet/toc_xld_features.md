## Features

#### List of Operators

- [`AreaCenterPointsXld`](area_center_points_xld.md) — Area and center of gravity (centroid) of contours and polygons
  treated as point clouds.

- [`AreaCenterXld`](area_center_xld.md) — Area and center of gravity (centroid) of contours and polygons.

- [`CircularityXld`](circularity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the circularity (similarity to a circle) of contours or
  polygons.

- [`CompactnessXld`](compactness_xld.md) — Shape factor for the compactness of contours or polygons.

- [`ContourPointNumXld`](contour_point_num_xld.md) — Return the number of points in an XLD contour.

- [`ConvexityXld`](convexity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the convexity of contours or polygons.

- [`DiameterXld`](diameter_xld.md) — Maximum distance between two contour or polygon points.

- [`DistEllipseContourPointsXld`](dist_ellipse_contour_points_xld.md) — Compute the distances of all contour points to an ellipse.

- [`DistEllipseContourXld`](dist_ellipse_contour_xld.md) — Compute the distance of contours to an ellipse.

- [`DistRectangle2ContourPointsXld`](dist_rectangle2_contour_points_xld.md) — Compute the distances of all contour points to a rectangle.

- [`EccentricityPointsXld`](eccentricity_points_xld.md) — Anisometry of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`EccentricityXld`](eccentricity_xld.md) — Shape features derived from the ellipse parameters of contours or polygons.

- [`EllipticAxisPointsXld`](elliptic_axis_points_xld.md) — Parameters of the equivalent ellipse of contours or polygons treated as
  point clouds.

- [`EllipticAxisXld`](elliptic_axis_xld.md) — Parameters of the equivalent ellipse of contours or polygons.

- [`FitCircleContourXld`](fit_circle_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by circles.

- [`FitEllipseContourXld`](fit_ellipse_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by ellipses or elliptic arcs.

- [`FitLineContourXld`](fit_line_contour_xld.md) — Approximate XLD contours by line segments.

- [`FitRectangle2ContourXld`](fit_rectangle2_contour_xld.md) — Fit rectangles to XLD contours.

- [`GetContourAngleXld`](get_contour_angle_xld.md) — Calculate the direction of an XLD contour for each contour point.

- [`GetContourAttribXld`](get_contour_attrib_xld.md) — Return point attribute values of an XLD contour.

- [`GetContourGlobalAttribXld`](get_contour_global_attrib_xld.md) — Return global attributes values of an XLD contour.

- [`GetRegressParamsXld`](get_regress_params_xld.md) — Return XLD contour parameters.

- [`HeightWidthRatioXld`](height_width_ratio_xld.md) — Compute the width, height, and aspect ratio of the enclosing
  rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes of contours or polygons.

- [`InfoParallelsXld`](info_parallels_xld.md) — Return information about the gray values of the area enclosed by XLD
  parallels.

- [`LengthXld`](length_xld.md) — Length of contours or polygons.

- [`LocalMaxContoursXld`](local_max_contours_xld.md) — Select XLD contours with a local maximum of gray values.

- [`MaxParallelsXld`](max_parallels_xld.md) — Join modified XLD parallels lying on the same polygon.

- [`MomentsAnyPointsXld`](moments_any_points_xld.md) — Arbitrary geometric moments of contours or polygons treated as point
  clouds.

- [`MomentsAnyXld`](moments_any_xld.md) — Arbitrary geometric moments of contours or polygons.

- [`MomentsPointsXld`](moments_points_xld.md) — Geometric moments M20, M02, and M11
  of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`MomentsXld`](moments_xld.md) — Geometric moments M20, M02, and
  M11 of contours or polygons.

- [`OrientationPointsXld`](orientation_points_xld.md) — Calculate the orientation of contours or polygons treated as point clouds.

- [`OrientationXld`](orientation_xld.md) — Calculate the orientation of contours or polygons.

- [`QueryContourAttribsXld`](query_contour_attribs_xld.md) — Return the names of the defined attributes of an XLD contour.

- [`QueryContourGlobalAttribsXld`](query_contour_global_attribs_xld.md) — Return the names of the defined global attributes of an XLD contour.

- [`RectangularityXld`](rectangularity_xld.md) — Shape factor for the rectangularity of contours or polygons.

- [`SelectContoursXld`](select_contours_xld.md) — Select XLD contours according to several features.

- [`SelectShapeXld`](select_shape_xld.md) — Select contours or polygons using shape features.

- [`SelectXldPoint`](select_xld_point.md) — Choose all contours or polygons containing a given point.

- [`SmallestCircleXld`](smallest_circle_xld.md) — Smallest enclosing circle of contours or polygons.

- [`SmallestRectangle1Xld`](smallest_rectangle1_xld.md) — Enclosing rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes of contours or polygons.

- [`SmallestRectangle2Xld`](smallest_rectangle2_xld.md) — Smallest enclosing rectangle with arbitrary orientation of contours or
  polygons.

- [`TestClosedXld`](test_closed_xld.md) — Test whether contours or polygons are closed.

- [`TestSelfIntersectionXld`](test_self_intersection_xld.md) — Test XLD contours or polygons for self intersection.

- [`TestXldPoint`](test_xld_point.md) — Test whether one or more contours or polygons enclose the given point(s).
