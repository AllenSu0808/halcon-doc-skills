## Wiener Filter

#### List of Operators

- [`GenPsfDefocus`](gen_psf_defocus.md) — Generate an impulse response of an uniform out-of-focus blurring.

- [`GenPsfMotion`](gen_psf_motion.md) — Generate an impulse response of a (linearly) motion blurring.

- [`SimulateDefocus`](simulate_defocus.md) — Simulate an uniform out-of-focus blurring of an image.

- [`SimulateMotion`](simulate_motion.md) — Simulation of (linearly) motion blur.

- [`WienerFilter`](wiener_filter.md) — Image restoration by Wiener filtering.
