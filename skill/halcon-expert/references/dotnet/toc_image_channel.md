## Channel

#### List of Operators

- [`AccessChannel`](access_channel.md) — Access a channel of a multi-channel image.

- [`AppendChannel`](append_channel.md) — Append additional matrices (channels) to the image.

- [`ChannelsToImage`](channels_to_image.md) — Convert one-channel images into a multi-channel image

- [`Compose2`](compose2.md) — Convert two images into a two-channel image.

- [`Compose3`](compose3.md) — Convert 3 images into a three-channel image.

- [`Compose4`](compose4.md) — Convert 4 images into a four-channel image.

- [`Compose5`](compose5.md) — Convert 5 images into a five-channel image.

- [`Compose6`](compose6.md) — Convert 6 images into a six-channel image.

- [`Compose7`](compose7.md) — Convert 7 images into a seven-channel image.

- [`CountChannels`](count_channels.md) — Count channels of image.

- [`Decompose2`](decompose2.md) — Convert a two-channel image into two images.

- [`Decompose3`](decompose3.md) — Convert a three-channel image into three images.

- [`Decompose4`](decompose4.md) — Convert a four-channel image into four images.

- [`Decompose5`](decompose5.md) — Convert a five-channel image into five images.

- [`Decompose6`](decompose6.md) — Convert a six-channel image into six images.

- [`Decompose7`](decompose7.md) — Convert a seven-channel image into seven images.

- [`ImageToChannels`](image_to_channels.md) — Convert a multi-channel image into One-channel images
