## Conversion

#### List of Operators

- [`handle_to_integer`](handle_to_integer.md) — Convert a handle into an integer.

- [`integer_to_handle`](integer_to_handle.md) — Convert an integer into a handle.

- [`tuple_chr`](tuple_chr.md) — Convert a tuple of integer numbers into strings.

- [`tuple_chrt`](tuple_chrt.md) — Convert a tuple of integer numbers into strings.

- [`tuple_int`](tuple_int.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`tuple_number`](tuple_number.md) — Convert a tuple (of strings) into a tuple of numbers.

- [`tuple_ord`](tuple_ord.md) — Convert a tuple of strings of length 1 into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`tuple_ords`](tuple_ords.md) — Convert a tuple of strings into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`tuple_real`](tuple_real.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of floating point numbers.

- [`tuple_round`](tuple_round.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of integer numbers.

- [`tuple_string`](tuple_string.md) — Convert a tuple into a tuple of strings.
