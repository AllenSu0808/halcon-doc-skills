## Serial

#### List of Operators

- [`clear_serial`](clear_serial.md) — Clear the buffer of a serial connection.

- [`close_serial`](close_serial.md) — Close a serial device.

- [`get_serial_param`](get_serial_param.md) — Get the parameters of a serial device.

- [`open_serial`](open_serial.md) — Open a serial device.

- [`read_serial`](read_serial.md) — Read from a serial device.

- [`set_serial_param`](set_serial_param.md) — Set the parameters of a serial device.

- [`write_serial`](write_serial.md) — Write to a serial connection.
