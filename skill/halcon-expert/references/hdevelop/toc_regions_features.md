## Features

List of Operators ↓

This chapter contains operators that allow you to access the different
features of regions.

#### List of Operators

- [`area_center`](area_center.md) — Area and center of regions.

- [`area_holes`](area_holes.md) — Compute the area of holes of regions.

- [`circularity`](circularity.md) — Shape factor for the circularity (similarity to a circle) of a region.

- [`compactness`](compactness.md) — Shape factor for the compactness of a region.

- [`connect_and_holes`](connect_and_holes.md) — Number of connection components and holes

- [`contlength`](contlength.md) — Contour length of a region.

- [`convexity`](convexity.md) — Shape factor for the convexity of a region.

- [`diameter_region`](diameter_region.md) — Maximal distance between two boundary points of a region.

- [`eccentricity`](eccentricity.md) — Shape features derived from the ellipse parameters.

- [`elliptic_axis`](elliptic_axis.md) — Calculate the parameters of the equivalent ellipse.

- [`euler_number`](euler_number.md) — Calculate the Euler number.

- [`find_neighbors`](find_neighbors.md) — Search direct neighbors.

- [`get_region_index`](get_region_index.md) — Index of all regions containing a given pixel.

- [`get_region_thickness`](get_region_thickness.md) — Access the thickness of a region along the main axis.

- [`hamming_distance`](hamming_distance.md) — Hamming distance between two regions.

- [`hamming_distance_norm`](hamming_distance_norm.md) — Hamming distance between two regions using normalization.

- [`height_width_ratio`](height_width_ratio.md) — Compute the width, height, and aspect ratio of the surrounding
  rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes.

- [`inner_circle`](inner_circle.md) — Largest inner circle of a region.

- [`inner_rectangle1`](inner_rectangle1.md) — Largest inner rectangle of a region.

- [`moments_region_2nd`](moments_region_2nd.md) — Calculate the geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_2nd_invar`](moments_region_2nd_invar.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_2nd_rel_invar`](moments_region_2nd_rel_invar.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_3rd`](moments_region_3rd.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_3rd_invar`](moments_region_3rd_invar.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_central`](moments_region_central.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`moments_region_central_invar`](moments_region_central_invar.md) — Geometric moments of regions.

- [`orientation_region`](orientation_region.md) — Orientation of a region.

- [`rectangularity`](rectangularity.md) — Shape factor for the rectangularity of a region.

- [`region_features`](region_features.md) — Calculate shape features of regions.

- [`roundness`](roundness.md) — Shape factors from contour.

- [`runlength_distribution`](runlength_distribution.md) — Distribution of runs needed for runlength encoding of a region.

- [`runlength_features`](runlength_features.md) — Characteristic values for runlength coding of regions.

- [`select_region_point`](select_region_point.md) — Choose all regions containing a given pixel.

- [`select_region_spatial`](select_region_spatial.md) — Pose relation of regions.

- [`select_shape`](select_shape.md) — Choose regions with the aid of shape features.

- [`select_shape_proto`](select_shape_proto.md) — Choose regions having a certain relation to each other.

- [`select_shape_std`](select_shape_std.md) — Select regions of a given shape.

- [`smallest_circle`](smallest_circle.md) — Smallest surrounding circle of a region.

- [`smallest_rectangle1`](smallest_rectangle1.md) — Surrounding rectangle parallel to the coordinate axes.

- [`smallest_rectangle2`](smallest_rectangle2.md) — Smallest surrounding rectangle with any orientation.

- [`spatial_relation`](spatial_relation.md) — Pose relation of regions with regard to the coordinate axes.
