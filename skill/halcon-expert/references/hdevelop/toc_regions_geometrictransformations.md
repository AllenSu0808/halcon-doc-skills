## Geometric Transformations

#### List of Operators

- [`affine_trans_region`](affine_trans_region.md) — Apply an arbitrary affine 2D transformation to regions.

- [`mirror_region`](mirror_region.md) — Reflect a region about an axis.

- [`move_region`](move_region.md) — Translate a region.

- [`polar_trans_region`](polar_trans_region.md) — Transform a region within an annular arc to polar coordinates.

- [`polar_trans_region_inv`](polar_trans_region_inv.md) — Transform a region in polar coordinates back to Cartesian
  coordinates.

- [`projective_trans_region`](projective_trans_region.md) — Apply a projective transformation to a region.

- [`transpose_region`](transpose_region.md) — Reflect a region about a point.

- [`zoom_region`](zoom_region.md) — Zoom a region.
