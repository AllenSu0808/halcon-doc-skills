## Creation

#### List of Operators

- [`clear_handle`](clear_handle.md) — Clear the content of a handle.

- [`tuple_concat`](tuple_concat.md) — Concatenate two tuples to a new one.

- [`tuple_constant`](tuple_constant.md) — Generate a tuple with the value of a HDevelop language constant.

- [`tuple_gen_const`](tuple_gen_const.md) — Generate a tuple of a specific length and initialize its elements.

- [`tuple_gen_sequence`](tuple_gen_sequence.md) — Generate a tuple with a sequence of equidistant values.

- [`tuple_rand`](tuple_rand.md) — Return a tuple of random numbers between 0 and 1.

- [`tuple_repeat`](tuple_repeat.md) — Repeat a tuple.

- [`tuple_repeat_elem`](tuple_repeat_elem.md) — Repeat the elements of a tuple.
