## Bit

#### List of Operators

- [`bit_and`](bit_and.md) — Bit-by-bit AND of all pixels of the input images.

- [`bit_lshift`](bit_lshift.md) — Left shift of all pixels of the image.

- [`bit_mask`](bit_mask.md) — Logical “AND” of each pixel using a bit mask.

- [`bit_not`](bit_not.md) — Complement all bits of the pixels.

- [`bit_or`](bit_or.md) — Bit-by-bit OR of all pixels of the input images.

- [`bit_rshift`](bit_rshift.md) — Right shift of all pixels of the image.

- [`bit_slice`](bit_slice.md) — Extract a bit from the pixels.

- [`bit_xor`](bit_xor.md) — Bit-by-bit XOR of all pixels of the input images.
