## Transformations

#### List of Operators

- [`add_noise_white_contour_xld`](add_noise_white_contour_xld.md) — Add noise to XLD contours.

- [`clip_contours_xld`](clip_contours_xld.md) — Clip an XLD contour.

- [`clip_end_points_contours_xld`](clip_end_points_contours_xld.md) — Clip the end points of an XLD contour.

- [`close_contours_xld`](close_contours_xld.md) — Close an XLD contour.

- [`combine_roads_xld`](combine_roads_xld.md) — Combine road hypotheses from two resolution levels.

- [`crop_contours_xld`](crop_contours_xld.md) — Crop an XLD contour.

- [`merge_cont_line_scan_xld`](merge_cont_line_scan_xld.md) — Merge XLD contours from successive line scan images.

- [`regress_contours_xld`](regress_contours_xld.md) — Calculate the parameters of a regression line to an XLD contour.

- [`segment_contour_attrib_xld`](segment_contour_attrib_xld.md) — Segment XLD contour parts whose local attributes fulfill given
  conditions.

- [`segment_contours_xld`](segment_contours_xld.md) — Segment XLD contours into line segments and circular or elliptic arcs.

- [`shape_trans_xld`](shape_trans_xld.md) — Transform the shape of contours or polygons.

- [`smooth_contours_xld`](smooth_contours_xld.md) — Smooth an XLD contour.

- [`sort_contours_xld`](sort_contours_xld.md) — Sort contours with respect to their relative position.

- [`split_contours_xld`](split_contours_xld.md) — Split XLD contours at dominant points.

- [`union_adjacent_contours_xld`](union_adjacent_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of contours whose end points are close together.

- [`union_cocircular_contours_xld`](union_cocircular_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of contours that belong to the same circle.

- [`union_collinear_contours_ext_xld`](union_collinear_contours_ext_xld.md) — Compute the union of collinear contours
  (operator with extended functionality).

- [`union_collinear_contours_xld`](union_collinear_contours_xld.md) — Unite approximately collinear contours.

- [`union_cotangential_contours_xld`](union_cotangential_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of cotangential contours.

- [`union_straight_contours_xld`](union_straight_contours_xld.md) — Compute the union of neighboring straight contours that have a similar
  direction.
