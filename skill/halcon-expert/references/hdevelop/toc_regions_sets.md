## Sets

#### List of Operators

- [`complement`](complement.md) — Return the complement of a region.

- [`difference`](difference.md) — Calculate the difference of two regions.

- [`intersection`](intersection.md) — Calculate the intersection of two regions.

- [`symm_difference`](symm_difference.md) — Calculate the symmetric difference of two regions.

- [`union1`](union1.md) — Return the union of all input regions.

- [`union2`](union2.md) — Return the union of two regions.
