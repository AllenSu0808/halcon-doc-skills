## Transformations

#### List of Operators

- [`background_seg`](background_seg.md) — Determine the connected components of the background of given regions.

- [`clip_region`](clip_region.md) — Clip a region to a rectangle.

- [`clip_region_rel`](clip_region_rel.md) — Clip a region relative to its smallest surrounding rectangle.

- [`closest_point_transform`](closest_point_transform.md) — Compute the closest-point transformation of a region.

- [`connection`](connection.md) — Compute connected components of a region.

- [`distance_transform`](distance_transform.md) — Compute the distance transformation of a region.

- [`eliminate_runs`](eliminate_runs.md) — Eliminate runs of a given length.

- [`expand_region`](expand_region.md) — Fill gaps between regions or split overlapping regions.

- [`fill_up`](fill_up.md) — Fill up holes in regions.

- [`fill_up_shape`](fill_up_shape.md) — Fill up holes in regions having given shape features.

- [`junctions_skeleton`](junctions_skeleton.md) — Find junctions and end points in a skeleton.

- [`merge_regions_line_scan`](merge_regions_line_scan.md) — Merge regions from line scan images.

- [`partition_dynamic`](partition_dynamic.md) — Partition a region horizontally at positions of small vertical extent.

- [`partition_rectangle`](partition_rectangle.md) — Partition a region into rectangles of approximately equal size.

- [`rank_region`](rank_region.md) — Rank operator for regions.

- [`remove_noise_region`](remove_noise_region.md) — Remove noise from a region.

- [`shape_trans`](shape_trans.md) — Transform the shape of a region.

- [`skeleton`](skeleton.md) — Compute the skeleton of a region.

- [`sort_region`](sort_region.md) — Sorting of regions with respect to their relative position.

- [`split_skeleton_lines`](split_skeleton_lines.md) — Split lines represented by one pixel wide, non-branching lines.

- [`split_skeleton_region`](split_skeleton_region.md) — Split lines represented by one pixel wide, non-branching regions.
